Write

Page 1 becomes the front cover. Empty pads fill the quire.

Order

No account. You get the sheet diagram and a numbered dummy to fold first.

Guide

Six pages on letter paper, one quire of 8. It explains the fold by being the fold.

How

1

Pick a quire. 4 is one sheet. 8 is a thin signature. 16 is a section you can sew.

2

The diagram shows each sheet, front and back. Page 1 sits on the front right. The last page of that quire sits on the front left.

3

Print landscape, double sided, flip on the short edge. Fold on the ticks. Nest the center sheet inside the outside sheet.

Limits

  • 64 pages. A saved booklet lasts 30 days, then it is gone.
  • 12 new booklets per IP per hour. The owner link is the only key.
  • No creep shift. Inner sheets may stick out. Trim after folding if you care.
  • No accounts. Lose the owner link and the booklet stays read only.
  • Script the order: /api/plan?pages=8&quire=8
